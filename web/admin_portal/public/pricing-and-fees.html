Transparent breakdown of platform fees and payout rules

ONLYGIGZ PRICING & FEES POLICY Last Updated: August 6, 2026 This Pricing & Fees Policy (“Policy”) explains the fees, optional paid features, escrow and payment timing, and payment terms applicable to users of the OnlyGigz platform (“Platform,” “OnlyGigz,” “we,” or “us”). This Policy is incorporated by reference into the OnlyGigz Terms and Conditions (the “Terms”). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms. By using the Platform, you agree to the pricing presented at the time of purchase or booking and to this Policy. 1. Overview OnlyGigz provides a marketplace connecting Entertainers and Organizers (“Users”). Certain features are available at no cost, while others require payment. Current pricing is always displayed within the Platform before any purchase, subscription, or booking is completed. Displayed pricing at the time of a transaction governs that transaction, notwithstanding any different figure that may appear elsewhere in the Platform or in marketing materials. 2. Subscription Plans OnlyGigz may offer multiple subscription plans for Entertainers and Organizers. Subscription plans may include additional features such as: • Reduced booking commissions • Unlimited applications or Gig postings • Advanced search and filtering • Calendar management tools • Premium profile features • Priority customer support • Analytics and reporting • Early access to new features Current subscription pricing and included features are displayed within the Platform and may change from time to time in accordance with Section 11 (Pricing Changes). 3. Booking Service Fees OnlyGigz may charge service fees on completed bookings. Applicable fees are displayed before a booking is confirmed and may vary based on: • User subscription level • Booking amount • Payment method

• Promotional offers • Geographic location • Other Platform factors Service fees are deducted or collected as disclosed at checkout. Because OnlyGigz uses staged escrow payments (see Section 5), the point at which a service fee is charged, held, or released does not always coincide with the date a Gig Contract is signed or the date the Gig is performed. Section 5 governs how fees are treated at each stage. 4. Payment Processing Fees Payments made through the Platform are securely processed by Stripe or another authorized payment processor using Stripe Connect. Payment processing fees may apply and will be disclosed before payment is submitted. OnlyGigz does not store complete payment card information. 5. Escrow, Fund Holds & Payment Timing OnlyGigz uses a staged escrow model to hold and release booking funds. Understanding this timeline is necessary to understand when fees are earned, held, or refundable. 5.1 Payment Stages • Authorization: When an Organizer and Entertainer execute a Gig Contract through the Platform, the Organizer's payment method is authorized (placed on hold) for the booking amount plus any applicable service fees. • Charge: The authorized amount is charged approximately five (5) to seven (7) days before the scheduled Gig date, unless a shorter window applies because the Gig Contract is signed closer to the performance date. • Release: Funds held in escrow are released to the Entertainer's connected Stripe account twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) hours after the scheduled performance, subject to confirmation of performance and any pending dispute under Section 6. 5.2 Fee Treatment by Stage • If a booking is cancelled prior to the Charge stage, no service fee has yet been collected and none will be charged for that booking. • If a booking is cancelled after the Charge stage but before the scheduled Gig, refund of the booking amount and any associated service fee is governed by the Gig Contract and the Platform's Cancellation & Refund Policy, not by this Section alone. • If a charge fails (e.g., a declined card or insufficient funds) during the Charge stage, OnlyGigz or its payment processor may retry the charge and will notify the Organizer. A Gig Contract may be treated as unfulfilled and subject to cancellation terms if payment cannot be collected before the scheduled performance. • Once funds are released to an Entertainer following a completed Gig, the associated service fee is deemed earned by OnlyGigz and is non-refundable except as required by law or as OnlyGigz determines appropriate in connection with a dispute under Section 6.

This Section 5 does not itself create a right to cancel or a refund. Cancellation eligibility is governed exclusively by the applicable Gig Contract and the Platform's Cancellation & Refund Policy; this Section only describes how fees are treated once a cancellation or refund has otherwise been determined to apply. 6. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes Users agree to contact OnlyGigz support to resolve a billing concern before initiating a chargeback, dispute, or reversal with their bank or card issuer. Because escrowed funds may already have been released to an Entertainer by the time a chargeback is filed, an improper or bad-faith chargeback can result in OnlyGigz absorbing funds that have already been paid out to another User. If a User initiates a chargeback or payment dispute: • OnlyGigz may suspend the User's account and any pending payouts pending investigation. • OnlyGigz may recoup the disputed amount, together with any processor chargeback fee, from the User's future payouts, from any Platform balance, or by charging the payment method on file, to the extent permitted by the User's agreement with their payment processor and applicable law. • Repeated or fraudulent chargebacks may result in account suspension or termination under the Terms. Nothing in this Section limits a User's rights under applicable consumer protection or payment card association rules. 7.
