[COLOR:0f172a] Executive Summary
This document outlines the legal & compliance requirements for the OnlyGigz platform, alongside operational, architectural, and financial questions identified through codebase analysis and client requirements.
The contents cover all four main system components:
[COLOR:2d3748] Musician Mobile App (apps/musician)
[COLOR:2d3748] Organizer Mobile App (apps/organizer)
[COLOR:2d3748] Web Admin Portal (web/admin_portal)
[COLOR:2d3748] Gig Scraper & Backend Engine (scraper & backend)
[COLOR:0f172a] Part 1: Requirements (Legal, Compliance & Policy Documents)
To launch and operate OnlyGigz legally and securely, the following documentation and policies must be drafted and integrated into all client-facing applications:
[COLOR:1e293b] 1. Terms & Conditions (T&C)
[COLOR:2d3748] User Accounts & Eligibility:  [COLOR:2d3748] Rules governing account creation, verification, age requirements, and profile accuracy for both musicians and event organizers.
[COLOR:2d3748] Platform Role:  [COLOR:2d3748] Explicit statement that OnlyGigz acts as a venue/marketplace facilitating connections between musicians and event organizers, outlining liability limits.
[COLOR:2d3748] Conduct & Acceptable Use:  [COLOR:2d3748] Rules against spam, harassment, circumvention of platform payments, and fraudulent gig postings.
[COLOR:1e293b] 2. Privacy Policy
[COLOR:2d3748] Data Collection & Usage:  [COLOR:2d3748] Disclosure of personal data collected (name, email, location, payment info, audio clips, portfolio media, device metadata).
[COLOR:2d3748] Third-Party Services:  [COLOR:2d3748] Transparent disclosure of integrated services including Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage), Stripe (Payments & Connect payouts), and OpenAI/Gemini (AI classification).
[COLOR:2d3748] Data Scraping Transparency:  [COLOR:2d3748] Informing users how publicly available gig data is ingested, processed, and displayed on the platform.
[COLOR:2d3748] Data Retention & Deletion:  [COLOR:2d3748] Instructions for users on how to request profile deletion and data export in compliance with privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA).
[COLOR:1e293b] 3. Terms of Service (ToS)
[COLOR:2d3748] Service Agreements & Bookings:  [COLOR:2d3748] Binding agreement terms between organizers and musicians upon gig acceptance.
[COLOR:2d3748] Cancellation & Refund Policies:  [COLOR:2d3748] Clear conditions for booking cancellations, refund eligibility, notice periods, and emergency no-shows.
[COLOR:2d3748] Payment & Escrow Terms:  [COLOR:2d3748] Terms detailing how funds are held prior to gig completion and released upon verification.
[COLOR:1e293b] 4. Copyright & Scraped Content Policy (DMCA / Fair Use)
[COLOR:2d3748] External Scraped Gigs:  [COLOR:2d3748] Legal notice explaining that scraped gig listings originate from public third-party sources (e.g., Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist).
[COLOR:2d3748] Takedown Request Mechanism:  [COLOR:2d3748] A clear process allowing original venue owners or gig posters to request removal of their scraped listing or claim ownership of their gig account on OnlyGigz.
[COLOR:1e293b] 5. Payment, Escrow & Payout Policy
[COLOR:2d3748] Platform Service Fees:  [COLOR:2d3748] Breakdown of service fees charged to musicians and organizers per transaction.
[COLOR:2d3748] Stripe Connect & Payout Terms:  [COLOR:2d3748] Requirements for Stripe onboarding, identity verification (KYC), bank payout schedules, and dispute/chargeback responsibilities.
[COLOR:1e293b] 6. About Us & Company Overview Document
[COLOR:2d3748] Store Submission & Compliance:  [COLOR:2d3748] Official company biography and mission statement required for Apple App Store and Google Play Store developer review and publication.
[COLOR:2d3748] User & Partner Transparency:  [COLOR:2d3748] Informational content explaining the OnlyGigz origin, core team background, marketplace values, and service offerings to users.

Focus / Impact: Scope and enforcement of 2FA across user roles.

Focus / Impact: Financial resolution controls for disputed escrow funds.
