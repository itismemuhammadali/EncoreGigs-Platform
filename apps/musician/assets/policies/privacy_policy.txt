This Privacy Policy explains how ONLYGIGZ, LLC (“OnlyGigz,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information through the OnlyGigz App, Admin Dashboard, and website (collectively, the “Platform”). It applies to Musicians, Organizers/Venues, and anyone else who visits or uses the Platform, and should be read together with our Terms and Conditions. This Policy covers information we collect through the Platform, through emails, texts, or other electronic messages between you and OnlyGigz, and when you interact with our ads or content on third-party sites that link back to this Policy. It does not cover information collected by any third party through their own websites, apps, or services, even if they link to or integrate with the Platform — including listings imported by the Aggregation Module, which are addressed separately in Section 6 below. By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Platform.

1. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. No one under 13 may provide information to the Platform. If we learn that a child has provided us personal information, we will delete it. If you believe we may have collected information from a child, contact us at admin@onlygigz.app .

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically as you use the Platform, and in some cases from third parties (for example, publicly available Gig postings imported, described in Section 6). The categories of information we collect include: ● Account & profile information: name, email, phone number, password, profile photo, bio, genre, skills/instruments, pricing, and (for Organizers) business/venue name and location. ● Portfolio content: performance photos, video clips, and audio samples you upload. ● Availability data: the dates and times you mark as available or unavailable, used by the Matching Engine and by Organizers when booking. ● Verification & tax documents: if you upload identity documents (government-issued ID), tax forms (e.g., 1099s, which may include a Social Security Number or Taxpayer ID), or certifications, we collect and securely store those files. This is sensitive information — see Section 3 for how it's protected. ● Payment & escrow information: billing and payout details, including bank or card information, are collected and stored directly by Stripe, our payment processor; OnlyGigz does not store full card numbers or bank account credentials. We retain transaction and escrow-status metadata (amount, dates, status, Booking reference) needed to operate the Platform. ● Gig Contracts: auto-generated contracts and the electronic signatures applied to them. ● Booking & communications data: messages between Musicians and Organizers, applications, Booking details, and reviews/ratings. ● Location data: approximate location (city/region) used for Gig matching and distance-based search; precise device location only if you enable it. ● Device & usage data: IP address, device type, operating system, app version, log data, crash reports, push-notification tokens, and how you interact with the Platform, including through cookies and similar technologies described in Section 8. Most of this information is provided directly by you — for example, when you register, complete your profile, apply to or post a Gig, upload a document, message another User, or contact us. Some information (device and usage data, approximate location, cookies) is collected automatically as you use the Platform. This information may be linked to your account or may be aggregated statistical data that does not identify you individually.

3. Sensitive Documents: How We Protect Them

Identity documents, tax forms, and certifications you upload are treated as sensitive information: ● Stored encrypted, separately from general profile data. ● Accessible only to authorized OnlyGigz Admin personnel for identity verification, tax/compliance, and fraud-prevention purposes — never shown to Organizers, other Musicians, or the public. ● Retained only as long as needed for verification and legal/tax recordkeeping purposes, and deleted on request subject to any legal retention obligations (for example, tax records that must be kept for a statutory period).

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to: ● Operate the Platform: account creation, profile display, Gig posting/browsing, Bookings, messaging, and notifications. ● Process payments and escrow, including facilitating payouts via Stripe and generating/storing Gig Contracts and e-signatures. ● Verify identity and tax status using uploaded documents, and maintain records for compliance. ● Power the Matching Engine, which recommends Gigs to Musicians and surfaces candidates to Organizers using profile, availability, genre, location, budget, and experience data. ● Run automated AI classification and spam/fraud detection on all Gig listings. ● Monitor for fraud, abuse, and Terms violations, and keep the Platform secure. ● Analyze and improve the Platform and deliver a more personalized experience, including estimating our user base and usage patterns. ● Send push notifications and other service communications; with your consent, marketing communications about OnlyGigz's own services. ● Comply with legal obligations, including tax reporting where applicable. If you do not want us to use your contact information to promote OnlyGigz's own services, you can adjust your notification and marketing preferences in your account settings, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. This does not affect service communications tied to your account or an active Booking.

5. Automated Matching & Fraud Screening

The Matching Engine and AI classification/spam-fraud detection systems process profile, availability, and Gig data to generate recommendations and flag suspicious listings automatically. These tools inform what you see (e.g., recommended Gigs) and support Admin review of imported listings, but Admin — not the algorithm alone — makes final decisions on account or listing actions with material consequences (e.g., removing a Gig or suspending an account).

6. Gig Listings from Public Sources

OnlyGigz automatically imports publicly available Gig postings from third-party public sources, including Eventbrite, Craigslist, and community or venue boards. These imported listings may include information about individuals or businesses (such as an event organizer's name or contact details) who have not created an OnlyGigz account. ● We only collect information that was already publicly posted by the source. ● This information is used to populate Gig listings and, where applicable, to create an Admin-issued account the relevant business or individual can later claim (see Terms, Section 2). If you are not an OnlyGigz User but believe your information was imported and want it corrected or removed, contact us at admin@onlygigz.app .

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information in these circumstances: ● With other Users: your profile, portfolio, availability, reviews, and (for confirmed Bookings) contact and messaging details are visible to the counterparty as part of the Platform's normal function. Verification documents are never shared with other Users. ● With Stripe: to process payments, escrow, and payouts, subject to Stripe's own privacy policy and terms. ● With e-signature and document-storage providers: to generate, sign, and securely store Gig Contracts and uploaded documents. ● With service providers: hosting, cloud storage, push-notification, analytics, and customer-support vendors who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. ● Aggregated or de-identified information: we may disclose statistics or other data that does not identify any individual without restriction. ● For legal reasons: to comply with law, respond to legal process, or protect the rights, safety, or property of OnlyGigz, our Users, or the public, including in connection with enforcing our Terms or investigating intellectual-property or fraud complaints. ● Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections. If we use analytics, advertising, or similar tracking partners that would qualify as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under applicable law (for example, disclosing device or usage identifiers to an ad partner for cross-context targeted advertising), you can opt out as described in Section 9 below. Any consent or opt-in you provide for text or push notifications will never be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Platform uses cookies, mobile device identifiers, web beacons/pixels, and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, understand usage (analytics), and measure the performance of in-app content. Web beacons and pixels are typically small, largely anonymous files used to count visits or confirm an email was opened; we and our service providers may also use them to understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser or device settings; disabling some may limit functionality, such as staying logged in or receiving personalized Gig recommendations.

9. Choices About How We Use and Disclose Your Information

You have the following controls over your information: ● Tracking technologies: adjust your browser or device settings to block or limit cookies and similar technologies, understanding that some Platform features may not work properly as a result. ● Marketing communications: opt out of promotional emails via the unsubscribe link, or adjust your notification preferences in the app. This does not opt you out of transactional or service-related messages tied to your account or Bookings. ● Push notifications and location: control permissions at any time through your device settings. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information To the extent OnlyGigz's use of analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies qualifies as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under the CCPA or a similar state law, you can opt out at any time by: ● Emailing admin@onlygigz.app with the subject line “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information,” or ● Enabling a recognized opt-out preference signal in your browser, such as Global Privacy Control (GPC), which we will honor as a valid opt-out request for that browser on that device. Once you opt out, we will not sell or share your personal information going forward unless you later opt back in. Opting out does not delete information already collected; use the deletion request process in Section 11 for that. We will not discriminate against you — for example, by denying service or charging a different rate — because you exercised this choice. Additional choices available to residents of certain states are described in Section 12 below.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law: ● Account, profile, and Booking data is retained while your account is active and as needed for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. ● Verification documents (IDs, tax forms, certifications) are retained only as long as needed for verification and compliance, per Section 3. ● Signed Gig Contracts and transaction/escrow records are retained for recordkeeping and dispute-resolution purposes, consistent with applicable financial and tax recordkeeping requirements. ● Device, usage, and cookie data is generally retained for shorter analytics-related periods unless needed for security or fraud investigation. Where information is used for more than one purpose, we retain it until the last applicable purpose no longer requires it.

11. Deletion of Your Personal Information

You can edit or delete most of your account information directly in the app. To request deletion of your account or other personal data we hold, contact us at admin@onlygigz.app . After verifying your request, we will delete the applicable information from our active records, subject to exceptions such as: ● Completing a transaction or Booking already in progress, or a service you've already paid for. ● Detecting, investigating, or preventing security incidents, fraud, or other malicious activity. ● Complying with a legal or tax recordkeeping obligation. ● Resolving an open dispute between Users. We cannot control, and are not responsible for, copies of your information that other Users may have already saved (for example, contact details exchanged during a completed Booking) before your deletion request. 12.
