4.  OnlyGigz's  Response  to  a  Notice  
When  OnlyGigz  receives  a  valid  or  substantially  compliant  notice,  it  may:  •  Remove  or  disable  access  to  the  identified  material;  •  Notify  the  user  or  account  holder  responsible  for  the  material;  •  Provide  that  person  with  a  copy  of  the  notice,  including  the  complainant's  contact  information;  •  Request  additional  information  from  either  party;  and  •  Document  the  notice  for  enforcement  of  the  repeat-infringer  policy.  OnlyGigz  may  remove  or  restrict  material  before  completing  its  review  when  reasonably  necessary.
