This Policy is intended to support OnlyGigz's procedures under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. section 512 (the "DMCA"). It applies to user-uploaded content, profiles, photographs, videos, audio, promotional materials, gig listings, messages where applicable, and third-party content displayed or imported through the Platform.

3. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner, or are authorized to act on behalf of one, and believe material available through the Platform infringes an exclusive right, send a written notice to the designated agent containing substantially all of the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed. If the notice covers multiple copyrighted works at a single online location, provide a representative list of those works.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing or the subject of infringing activity, together with information reasonably sufficient for OnlyGigz to locate it. Provide the specific profile, listing, post, media item, or URL whenever available.
  4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right allegedly infringed.

OnlyGigz may request additional information when a notice is incomplete, unclear, or does not identify the material with enough specificity. A copyright complaint should address copyright ownership and infringement; it should not be used solely to dispute the accuracy of a gig listing, ownership of a business profile, trademark rights, privacy, defamation, or another non-copyright issue.

4. OnlyGigz's Response to a Notice

When OnlyGigz receives a valid or substantially compliant notice, it may:

  • Remove or disable access to the identified material;
  • Notify the user or account holder responsible for the material;
  • Provide that person with a copy of the notice, including the complainant's contact information;
  • Request additional information from either party; and
  • Document the notice for enforcement of the repeat-infringer policy.

OnlyGigz may remove or restrict material before completing its review when reasonably necessary. Removal under this process is not a determination by a court that infringement occurred, and OnlyGigz does not adjudicate disputes concerning copyright ownership.

5. Counter-Notification

If material you submitted was removed or disabled because of a DMCA notice and you believe the removal resulted from mistake or misidentification, you may send a written counter-notification to the designated agent containing substantially all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal or disabling.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, together with a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the United States Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located, or, if your address is outside the United States, for any judicial district in which OnlyGigz may be found, and that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notice or that person's agent.

OnlyGigz may forward the counter-notification, including the information it contains, to the person who submitted the original notice.

6. Restoration After a Counter-Notification

After receiving a valid counter-notification, OnlyGigz may provide a copy to the original complainant and inform that person that the removed material may be restored. Unless the designated agent receives notice that the complainant has filed a court action seeking to restrain the allegedly infringing activity, OnlyGigz may restore the material or cease disabling access no fewer than 10 and no more than 14 business days after receiving the counter-notification, as provided by the DMCA. OnlyGigz may decline to restore material when it independently violates the Terms and Conditions, another Platform policy, applicable law, or the rights or safety of others, even if the requirements for a DMCA counter-notification are otherwise satisfied.

7. Repeat Infringer Policy

OnlyGigz maintains a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, users or account holders who are repeat infringers. OnlyGigz may consider valid copyright notices, counter-notifications, retractions, court orders, the nature of the conduct, and other relevant circumstances. OnlyGigz may also suspend, restrict, or terminate an account after a single serious or willful infringement and may prevent a terminated user from creating another account.

8. Misrepresentations and Abuse

The DMCA provides that a person who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing, or that material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Do not submit a takedown notice or counter-notification without a good-faith basis. OnlyGigz may restrict access to its reporting process or take action against accounts that misuse it.

9. Other Content Concerns

Requests concerning an inaccurate or outdated imported gig listing, a claimable business profile, personal information, trademark use, impersonation, or other non-copyright concerns should be sent to admin@onlygigz.app and should clearly identify the listing or content at issue and the requested correction or removal. OnlyGigz may route a submission to the process it considers appropriate.
