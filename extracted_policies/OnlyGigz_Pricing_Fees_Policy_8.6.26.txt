Escrow, Fund Holds & Payment Timing OnlyGigz uses a staged escrow model to hold and release booking funds. Understanding this timeline is necessary to understand when fees are earned, held, or refundable. 5.1 Payment Stages • Authorization: When an Organizer and Entertainer execute a Gig Contract through the Platform, the Organizer's payment method is authorized (placed on hold) for the booking amount plus any applicable service fees. • Charge: The authorized amount is charged approximately five (5) to seven (7) days before the scheduled Gig date, unless a shorter window applies because the Gig Contract is signed closer to the performance date. • Release: Funds held in escrow are released to the Entertainer's connected Stripe account twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) hours after the scheduled performance, subject to confirmation of performance and any pending dispute under Section

6.

5.2 Fee Treatment by Stage • If a booking is cancelled prior to the Charge stage, no service fee has yet been collected and none will be charged for that booking. • If a booking is cancelled after the Charge stage but before the scheduled Gig, refund of the booking amount and any associated service fee is governed by the Gig Contract and the Platform's Cancellation & Refund Policy, not by this Section alone. • If a charge fails (e.g., a declined card or insufficient funds) during the Charge stage, OnlyGigz or its payment processor may retry the charge and will notify the Organizer. A Gig Contract may be treated as unfulfilled and subject to cancellation terms if payment cannot be collected before the scheduled performance. • Once funds are released to an Entertainer following a completed Gig, the associated service fee is deemed earned by OnlyGigz and is non-refundable except as required by law or as OnlyGigz determines appropriate in connection with a dispute under Section
